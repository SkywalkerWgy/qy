[Failed Property]
preservation of i_7: assuming the invariant and loop condition before one iteration, the invariant must hold again after the loop body.

[Failure Cause]
The failed invariant i_7 is too weak / non-inductive for the loop body. After swapping a[dad_0] with a[dad_1], the old dad cell dad_0 receives a[dad_1]. For the parent of dad_0, the pre-state i_7 only gives that the parent is >= the old value at dad_0; it does not give that the parent is >= the promoted child value a[dad_1]. Thus after the swap, the parent of dad_0 may no longer dominate its child at dad_0. The max3 postcondition only helps re-establish the heap property at dad_0 itself, not the heap property at dad_0's parent.

[Responsible Scope]
A@_heapify

[Refinement Direction]
The missing logical fact is the ancestor/path domination fact needed to discharge the i = parent(dad_0) case: for every ancestor of the current dad, in particular parent(dad_0), its value must be at least the value of the child selected by max3, i.e. a[parent(dad_0)] >= a[dad_1]. This fact is not implied by i_7 and is exactly what bridges the available assumptions to the WP target.
