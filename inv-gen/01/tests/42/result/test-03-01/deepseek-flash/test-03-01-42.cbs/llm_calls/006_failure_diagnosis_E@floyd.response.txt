The preservation of i_24 cannot be proven because the available invariants do not state that the inner loop body only decreases distances. To re-establish i_24 after an update of `dist[i][j]`, the prover must know that the new value at any cell is `<=` its old value, so that the upper bound `dist[a][b] <= graph[a][m] + graph[m][b]` (which held before the update) remains true after the update. Without this monotonicity and the associated frame condition (other cells unchanged), the solver cannot relate the post-update state to the pre-update state that satisfied i_24.
