[Failed Property]
preservation of i_12: assuming the invariant and loop condition before one iteration, the invariant must hold again after the loop body.

[Failure Cause]
The preservation of `output_edge_order` after appending `v` requires proving two cases:  
1. For the new position `count`, every predecessor of `v` must already appear in `result[0..count-1]`. This is supplied by the inner-loop invariant `i_23`.  
2. For every old position `pos_to < count`, the newly appended vertex `v` must not introduce a new violation. Specifically, if there were an edge `v -> result[pos_to]`, then `v` would have to appear earlier than `pos_to`, which is impossible since `v` is appended at position `count > pos_to`. Hence one must know that `v` has no outgoing edge to any already output vertex.

The inner-loop invariants of `C@topo_sort` only reason about incoming edges to `v` (invariant `i_23`). They do not establish that `v` has no outgoing edges to vertices already present in `result`. Without this fact, the old positions of `output_edge_order` cannot be shown to remain valid when `v` is appended. The missing link is the consequence of invariant `i_20` (or `i_13`/`i_24` combined with `in_degree[v] != -1`) that for every already processed vertex `w` (i.e. `in_degree[w] == -1`), `graph[v][w] == 0`.

[Responsible Scope]
C@topo_sort

[Refinement Direction]
The inner loop `C@topo_sort` is too weak: its invariants must also capture the fact that the candidate vertex `v` has no outgoing edges to vertices that are already in the output prefix. In particular, the proof needs the fact that for all `w` with `in_degree[w] == -1`, `graph[v][w] == 0` (which follows from `i_20` and the branch condition `in_degree[v] != -1`). Since this fact is not part of the available assumptions after the inner loop terminates, the preservation of `i_12` cannot be discharged.
