This fact is not available, so the solver cannot relate the updated `sum_c` to the old `sum_c`.  
The invariant itself is not too strong; it is the intended specification. It is simply not inductive without the missing `sum_c` lemmas. The invariants of A@counting_sort are not too weak—they provide the needed pointwise zeros—but they do not supply the recursive sum reasoning.
